# Ramming up the Productivity

## Idea

A Pomodoro website to help students stay focused during study sessions, keep track of tasks, and foster friendly competition among peers to see who can be the most productive.

## Features

### 1. Timer
Start, pause, and reset a timer for studying or taking breaks.

- **User:** A student starting a study session.
- **Purpose:** Studying is split into chunks to prevent burnout.

### 2. Task List
Check off, add, and delete tasks from a list.

- **User:** Students planning their work.
- **Purpose:** Helps users see what's done and what's left.

### 3. Leaderboard
A productivity leaderboard against other users, based on average tasks completed per hour.

- **User:** A student who wants to compete with friends.
- **Purpose:** Adds friendly competition to studying for motivation.

### 4. Alarm Sound
Change the sound of the alarm.

- **User:** Students using the timer.
- **Purpose:** Lets them pick an alarm they enjoy hearing during studying.

### 5. Theme
Change the theme/background image.

- **User:** Students who want to customize the site.
- **Purpose:** Lets them make the study space more enjoyable.

## Backend Technology

- **Database:** Stores users' average time and their connections/friends on the site. This information is used to construct the leaderboard.
- **Authentication:** Email and password, with the user also entering a username.
- **Storage:** Saves the user's timers, tasks, and app theme.
